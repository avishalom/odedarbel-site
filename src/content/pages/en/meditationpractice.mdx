For thousands of years, across many spiritual traditions, people have sat in simple, present-moment awareness.

Meditation is a simple practice, though not always an easy one — a commitment to sit for a set period and observe without judgment or reaction.

Sitting with a group and a guide brings real advantages: it strengthens commitment and makes it possible to work through difficulties through shared conversation.

**Sangha** — a group of people practicing together and supporting one another through learning and growth.

Two active meditation groups — in Tel Aviv and in Moshav Tal Shahar.
