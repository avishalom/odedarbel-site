Senior partner in the teaching team of the Therapists Program. Clinical psychologist, graduate of Ben-Gurion and Reichman Universities, and graduate of the psychedelic psychotherapy program. Practices psychotherapy at clinics in Tel Aviv and Tal Shahar.

Facilitates groups, retreats, and personal-development processes focused on psychological resilience. Integrates psychodynamic understanding with Buddhist and shamanic approaches, incorporating body awareness, breath, and voice work.

Creates and holds safe spaces, participates in psychedelic research studies in Israel, and completed extended training with shamanic teachers in Mexico.

Interested in accompanying processes of maturation and growth for individuals.
