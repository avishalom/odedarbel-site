Mindfulness-based treatments are increasingly popular for a wide range of psychological and physical conditions.

## Services

- Mindfulness learning and practice groups
- Continuation groups
- Staff training on the therapeutic potential of mindfulness
- Burnout prevention and treatment
- Individual and group sessions

## Treatment areas

Chronic pain, stress and anxiety, depression and relapse prevention, PTSD and obsessive-compulsive disorders, personality and eating disorders, ADHD, psychotic conditions, and addictions.

## Course structure

8–10 sessions (1.5–2 hours each), combining theoretical background, guided meditation, and discussion of experience. Requires a commitment to independent practice at home.
