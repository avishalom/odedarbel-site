A three-year therapist training program run by the Desert Mindfulness Center, active since 2011. The curriculum combines Buddhist-inspired experiential psychotherapy with theoretical study, practical application, and clinical training in mindfulness-based approaches.

## Program structure

- One weekly study day (Wednesdays) plus intensive retreat periods
- Location: Kibbutz Hatzerim, in a comfortable, well-ventilated hall
- In-person attendance required; Zoom participation available in special cases

## Teaching philosophy

"The central therapeutic tool is therapeutic presence" — from which the clinical practices grow. Training includes several retreats to deepen learning and develop qualities of acceptance, presence, and compassion.

## Faculty

Led by Dr. Oded Arbel (psychiatrist and program director), alongside prominent teachers in Buddhist studies, somatic psychology, and meditation practice from Israel's academic and therapeutic communities.

## Who it's for

Mental-health professionals — psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, physicians, and creative-arts therapists — with prior psychodynamic training. Admission requires a personal interview.

## Annual cost

- Three-year program: 8,000 NIS per year
- Graduate continuation program: 4,500 NIS per year
